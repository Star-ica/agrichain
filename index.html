How it works
From field to buyer in four steps
01

Register as Farmer

Connect your wallet and register your name and farm location. An admin verifies your account on-chain.

02

Create a Lot

Mint a produce lot with crop details, weight, harvest date, and price per kg in BOT wei.

03

Add Checkpoints

Handlers add supply chain checkpoints as the lot moves — quality checks, transit, cold storage.

04

Buyer Purchases

Buyers browse the marketplace, pay in BOT, and ownership transfers on-chain instantly.
